# Quadrtic Surface
- Know: Conic sections
Formulas/shapes: (to put into anki cards w/ function form + graph view)
- Ellipsoid:
    - $\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2+\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2+\left( \frac{z}{c} \right)^2=1$
- cone
    - $\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2+\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2=\left( \frac{z}{c} \right)^2$
- hyberboloid of 1-sheet
    - $\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2+\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2-\left( \frac{z}{c} \right)^2=1$
- hyberboloid of 2-sheet
    - $-\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2-\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2+\left( \frac{z}{c} \right)^2=1$
- elliptic parabolid
    - $\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2+\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2=\frac{z}{c}$
- hyperbolic parabolid
    - $\left( \frac{x}{a} \right)^2-\left( \frac{y}{b} \right)^2=\frac{z}{c}$
