# Quiz! 
- $f(x,y) = e^{-x^2-y^2}$
- Find plane tangent to $f(x,y)_{x}$
- Find the direction of greatest ascent of given tangent plane 

- Let $c=-x^2-y^2$
- We find $f_{x}(x,y)=-2xe^c$, $f_{y}(x,y)=-2ye^c$
- Turn $f_{x}=S_{x}(s,t) \rightarrow <s,t,-2se^{-s^2-t^2}>$
- We then take partials of s and t
- Take cross product of both first partials, some $n$
