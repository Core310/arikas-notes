# Paper review
Each student needs to select and review a research paper published between 2020-2025 at one of the following conferences (must be main conference, not workshops)  
- AAAI, UAI, ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, KDD, ICRA, SODA  
A two-page review report needs be submitted by Dec 12, 2025. It should include a summary of the paper,  its main contribution or novelty. Any format is acceptable.

- main technical idea or specifics, 
- experimental or theoretical results 
- strength/weakness
